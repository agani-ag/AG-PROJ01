To send to a group

  1. Add {% if cfg.telegram_bot_username %}@{{ cfg.telegram_bot_username }}{% else %}our bot{% endif %} to the Telegram group.
  2. Click Scan for chats below. The group appears in the list with its chat id.
  3. Send that chat_id to POST /partner/v1/telegram/send.

To send to one person

  1. The person opens the bot link or scans the QR code and taps Start.
  2. Click Scan for chats. They appear in the list below.
Checks Telegram for new groups and people, and updates the list below.
