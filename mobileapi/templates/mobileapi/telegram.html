To send to a group

  1. Add @{{ cfg.telegram_bot_username }} to the Telegram group.
  2. Click Discover chats below. The group appears with its chat id.
  3. Send that chat_id to POST /partner/v1/telegram/send.

To send to one person

  1. The person opens the bot link or scans the QR code and taps Start.
  2. Click Discover chats. Their chat id appears.
