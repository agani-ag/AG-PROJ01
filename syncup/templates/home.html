{% icon "zap" "icon-sm" %}How it works

Up and running in three steps

  1. Install SyncUp

    Get it from Google Play, or scan the QR code above with your phone.

  2. Sign in

    Use the email and password your administrator gave you. SyncUp accounts are issued by your organization.

  3. Stay in sync

    Your links, reminders and messages are ready, and they update on their own as things change.
