How it works
  1. Play your music in a browser tab (YouTube, Spotify web, a local file — anything).
  2. Click Start → choose that tab → tick Share tab audio.
  3. Send friends the public link. They tap Listen and hear your audio live.
  4. Same Wi-Fi works out of the box. Remote friends connect too when routers allow (add a TURN server later for guaranteed remote).
